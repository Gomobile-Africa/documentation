A contact represents a customer, identified primarily by phone number. Standard fields and custom attributes provide the data your flows and audience rules use.

For an appointment reminder, a contact might have a name, phone number, and an appointment date stored in a custom attribute. Creating the record does not contact that person.

Imports match existing records by primary phone number and can update them. Review updates before importing.
