Gomobile is a customer-engagement platform for teams that run outbound campaigns, receive inbound calls, automate multichannel journeys, and use AI voice agents. A phone number is the primary identifier for a contact.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Manage customer data" icon="contact">
    Create contacts, add custom attributes, import lists, and review a contact's interaction history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build audiences" icon="users-round">
    Choose contacts manually with a static audience or use rules to maintain a live audience automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automate journeys" icon="git-branch">
    Build Voice and Multichannel flows with supported voice steps, conditions, contact updates, tags, calls, SMS, and approved WhatsApp templates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run campaigns" icon="send">
    Schedule an audience and flow, respect calling hours, configure eligible retries, and monitor outcomes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Handle inbound voice" icon="phone-incoming">
    Route an available phone number to an AI agent or Voice flow and review its history and results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Configure AI agents" icon="bot">
    Define the agent's instructions, welcome message, voice setup, and compatible knowledge base, then publish it for inbound use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage WhatsApp messages" icon="message-circle">
    Create templates, submit them for approval, and personalize messages with variables. Test approved templates and send them through campaigns and flows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understand activity" icon="activity">
    Investigate runs, calls, outcomes, timelines, transcripts and recordings when present, funnels, and credit usage.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What each channel supports

| Channel | Available customer workflows |
| --- | --- |
| Voice | Voice flows, campaign calls, inbound routing, separately configured AI agents, DTMF, audio playback, speech, forwarding, recordings, calls, and reports |
| SMS | Send an SMS from a supported flow using an available sender ID |
| WhatsApp | Manage templates and send an approved template from a supported flow |
| Multichannel | Combine conditions, contact changes, tags, SMS, WhatsApp templates, and a Voice sub-flow |

<Info>
  You can create SMS-only, WhatsApp-only, or combined campaigns using a Multichannel flow. Configure SMS and WhatsApp messages as actions within the flow, and add a Voice sub-flow when calls are needed.
</Info>

## Workspace administration

Manage your organization's calling hours, account security, API keys, and credit balance. Administrators can require two-step verification for organization members.

Before launching a campaign, check that your organization has the phone numbers, SMS sender IDs, or WhatsApp connection needed for the channels you plan to use.
