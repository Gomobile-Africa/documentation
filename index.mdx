## A practical starting path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Create contacts individually or import a customer list. Add custom attributes when you need business-specific data such as preferred branch, contract type, or renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to reach">
    Create a static audience that you maintain yourself, or a live audience whose membership follows rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the experience">
    Use a Voice flow for an in-call journey or a Multichannel flow to combine conditions, contact updates, tags, messaging, and a call sub-flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before launch">
    Run the saved flow against your verified number or a test contact. Tests perform real channel activity and may use credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and measure">
    Connect the audience and flow in a campaign, then use campaign results, runs, and calls to understand what happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Testing and launching can place real calls, send real messages, create customer and activity records, and consume credits. Use approved test contacts and channel resources, then check the target, caller or sender, flow values, and available credits before you continue.
</Warning>
