A campaign applies a supported flow to the contacts enrolled from an audience. Use this checklist to move from a tested flow to a controlled launch.

## Before you begin

Confirm that you have:

- a static or live audience with the intended contacts;
- a saved and tested Voice or Multichannel flow;
- an available phone number when the selected flow calls directly or invokes a Voice sub-flow;
- enough credits for the intended activity;
- approved organization or campaign-specific calling hours;
- consent and operational approval required by your organization.

<Warning>
  Review the audience and flow carefully. Their binding cannot be replaced after campaign creation. A static audience launches from a membership snapshot, while a live audience can continue enrolling contacts that match its rules while the campaign runs.
</Warning>

## Create the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new campaign">
    Open **Campaigns** and choose the action to create a campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add general information">
    Give the campaign a clear name and objective. Use a naming convention that helps operators distinguish region, purpose, and launch period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the audience and flow">
    Select the audience to enroll and the saved Voice or Multichannel flow to run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the schedule">
    Set the start and optional stop timing. Review the displayed timezone before confirming operational times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose calling hours">
    For both Voice and Multichannel campaigns, choose organization hours, define custom hours, or deliberately choose unrestricted operation if your policy allows it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign phone numbers">
    If voice is required, select the available number or numbers shown by Gomobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure eligible retries">
    Voice campaigns can use a fixed delay and maximum attempts or specific retry dates. Maximum attempts includes the initial call. Keep every retry within the campaign's active period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Before launch, review the current audience count, flow, timing, phone numbers, calling hours, and retry plan. After activation, check the enrolled-contact count; it can differ from the earlier audience count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or launch">
    Save a draft when the operation still needs review. Launch only when every setting and prerequisite is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Contact hours apply to messaging as well as voice initiation. Messages can wait for the next permitted window. Actions within an existing live call can be exempt; these settings do not establish legal compliance.

## After launch

Open the campaign detail to monitor status and progress. Depending on its state, you can pause, resume, stop, or remove the campaign using the actions Gomobile makes available.

- **Pause** prevents new campaign work from starting. Depending on timing, work or retries already scheduled may still proceed.
- **Resume** allows an eligible paused campaign to start new work again.
- **Stop** cancels pending campaign work. Calls already in progress may finish.

See [Understand the campaign lifecycle](/product/campaign-lifecycle) before managing a running campaign.

Use these views for investigation:

- **Campaign results** for enrolled contacts, settled progress, outcome buckets, tries, channel results, funnel, agent metrics, sub-flow reports, and credits.
- **Runs** for the automation path and values used for an individual contact.
- **Calls** for a voice interaction's source, outcome, timeline, transcript and recordings when present, and cost.

## Expected result

The campaign enters the selected lifecycle state. When active and inside its allowed schedule, eligible contacts begin progressing through the selected flow.

## If the campaign does not start

- Confirm its start time has arrived and its stop time has not passed.
- Check that the current time is inside the selected calling hours.
- Confirm the audience contains eligible contacts.
- Verify that the flow is saved and valid.
- Confirm any required phone number is assigned.
- Check available credits.
- Review the campaign detail for its current status and any visible failure reason.
