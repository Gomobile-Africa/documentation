Use a Voice flow to perform one controlled channel test.

## Before you begin

Set up your [account](/getting-started/set-up-account), prepare a test contact with a number you control, and check your credits. You need an available caller number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the flow">
    In Flows, create a **Voice flow** and give it a recognizable test name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the action">
    Add a **Text to speech** step, review the voice settings, and connect the call path. Use a message such as “This is a Gomobile test call.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and review">
    Connect the intended path and save. Resolve any validation errors before testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the saved flow">
    Open Test and choose your test contact or verified number. Choose Call the contact and an available caller ID. Confirm the destination and start the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the run">
    Check the resulting run timeline and the call in Calls. Confirm that the intended step executed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expected result

Your test phone rings and plays the configured message when you answer. The run records the automation path.

## Troubleshooting

Save changes before testing, supply all values the flow reads, and check the required channel resource and credits. Investigate the run’s failure details if the action does not complete.

A test performs real channel activity and may consume credits. See [build and test a flow](/getting-started/build-first-flow) for the complete testing process.
